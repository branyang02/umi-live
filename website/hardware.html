STEP 02

Print the UMI-Live components

  1. Download the STL files and keep their supplied print orientation. Use the STEP assembly to inspect or modify parts.
  2. Use the defaults in the 3D Printed tab, including a 0.4 mm nozzle, and follow each part's material, support, brim, and slicer settings. Some parts require bridging.
  3. Print the listed quantities and remove all supports. Use the illustrated parts list in the assembly guide to identify each part.

STEP 03

Assemble, wire, and inspect

Follow the assembly guide in order, including its wiring diagrams, power checks, and final checklist.

You will need M2/M3 drivers, scissors, flush cutters, a multimeter, and a wire stripper. Keep the carriages on the rail and disconnect the battery before assembly or wiring changes.

Print and fit the ArUco tags

Print the tag PDF at 100% / actual size, with Fit and Shrink disabled. Check the 50 mm scale before cutting and keep the white margins. Follow the assembly guide for placement and IDs.

Charge the battery

The battery's two bare-wire leads power the WAGO harness. Its barrel socket is for the supplied charger; the harness's separate barrel output powers the Airy.

  1. Switch OFF. Disconnect all connections to the battery, including both bare-wire leads from the WAGO harness.
  2. Plug the supplied charger directly into the battery's charging barrel socket.
  3. Unplug the charger before reconnecting the battery to the harness.

Do not charge and use the battery at the same time.

ROBOT-SIDE HARDWARE

Build the YAM gripper and camera mount

For each YAM gripper, print one camera mount, one left and one right finger holder, two finger backbones, and two pads.

The YAM folder contains the mount and holders. Use the shared UMI-Live files for the backbones and pads. Follow the I2RT YAM Parts settings below; its quantities and filament totals are separate from the handheld build.
